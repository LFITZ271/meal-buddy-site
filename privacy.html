Meal Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Meal Buddy doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What Meal Buddy stores

The foods, recipes, meal logs, goals, meal plans, budgets, pantry amounts and shopping lists you create are saved only on your device. There are no accounts, and Meal Buddy has no servers, so nothing you enter is sent to us or anyone else.

Like other app data, this information is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on; those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by Meal Buddy. Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone.

No tracking, analytics or ads

Meal Buddy contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Permissions, and how they're used

Camera
Only when you choose Scan label → Take photo. The photo is read on your iPhone (using Apple's on-device text recognition) to fill in the nutrition values, then discarded. It isn't saved or sent anywhere.
Photos
When you choose Scan label → Choose photo, iOS shows its photo picker and Meal Buddy only receives the one photo you pick. It's read on your iPhone and not saved.
Contacts
When you share a recipe and tap the contact button, iOS shows its contact picker. Meal Buddy only receives the phone number you tap, uses it to fill in the message, and doesn't save it. Meal Buddy can't read your contacts list.
Reminders
Only when you tap Add to Reminders on a shopping list. Meal Buddy creates a new list with your shopping items. It doesn't read or change your other reminders.
Messages
When you send a recipe, Meal Buddy opens the standard iOS message screen with the recipe card and recipe file attached. You choose whether to send it, and it goes through Apple's Messages like any text you send.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Meal Buddy.

Sharing recipes

A shared recipe contains what's in that recipe: its name, description, steps, and each ingredient's nutrition, price, package size and store. It's only sent to the people you choose, by the method you choose (Messages, AirDrop, Mail and so on). When you open a recipe someone sent you, it's added only to your device.

Children

Meal Buddy isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
