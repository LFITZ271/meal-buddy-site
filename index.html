Common questions

How do I add a food?

In Pantry, tap + and enter the details from the package's Nutrition Facts label, plus the price and the store you buy it from. Or tap Scan label to read the label with your camera; the nutrition values are filled in for you to check, and you add the name, price and store.

Why does the scanner miss some values?

Scanning works best with the Nutrition Facts panel flat, in good light and filling the frame. Curved or shiny packaging can be hard to read. Anything the scanner can't read is left blank for you to type in.

How does "on hand" work?

On a food's page, use Update amount to set what you have, or I bought… to add packages. Marking a recipe as made, logging a single food, and checking items off a shopping list all update the amount. Every change is listed on the food's page with an Undo button. Mark staples you never want counted, like olive oil, as Always have.

How do I plan a week of meal prep?

In Meal Prep, tap Add on a recipe and use + and − to set how many batches. Tap the Week of label to jump to any week. When you've cooked a recipe, tap Mark as made to take its ingredients out of your pantry.

How is the weekly budget worked out?

Budget adds up the packages you need to buy for that week's prep, skipping anything you already have on hand and anything marked Always have. Money you don't spend rolls into the next week (you can turn that off or set a limit in History). In History you can also enter what you actually spent.

How do shopping lists work?

From Budget, tap Shopping list. Items are grouped by store and in aisle order. Checking an item off adds it to your pantry; tap an item to update its shelf price. You can share a store's list as text or add it to Reminders.

How do I share a recipe?

Open a recipe and tap the share button, then type a phone number or pick a contact and tap Send in Messages. The message includes a recipe card anyone can read and a recipe file. If the person has Meal Buddy, opening the file shows the recipe with an Add to my recipes button.

"Send in Messages" is greyed out.

Your iPhone needs to be able to send texts with attachments (Messages turned on and a SIM or iMessage set up). You can still use Share another way to send the card and recipe file by AirDrop, Mail or another app.

Is my data backed up? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Meal Buddy keeps everything on your iPhone. It's included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings it along. Meal Buddy doesn't sync between devices.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual foods and recipes from their Edit screens, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. Meal Buddy has no servers, so there's nothing stored anywhere else.
